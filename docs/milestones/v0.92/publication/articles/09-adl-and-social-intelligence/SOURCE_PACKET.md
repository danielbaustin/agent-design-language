# Source Packet: ADL and Social Intelligence

## Brief

- Audience: multi-agent researchers, governance designers, and human-computer interaction practitioners.
- Promise: explain social intelligence as explicit, uncertain, privacy-bounded model state.
- Angle: useful coordination requires models of others, but those models must never become hidden authority.
- Series role: connect the runtime polis to bounded Theory of Mind.

## Claim Posture

- Current: a bounded Theory of Mind foundation exists as typed runtime state with evidence, uncertainty, correction, and visibility controls.
- Current but bounded: it supports hypotheses about others, not mind-reading, complete social cognition, or policy authority.
- Planned: broader reputation, relationship, social-learning, and governance-facing projections.

## Article Shape

1. Coordination requires representing what others may know or intend.
2. Evidence-bound hypotheses rather than narrative certainty.
3. Uncertainty, correction, privacy, and visibility.
4. Why inferred state cannot grant execution authority.
5. From a foundation toward a governed polis.

## Guardrails

Do not claim mind-reading, consciousness detection, unrestricted private-state access, reputation scoring, or complete social intelligence.
