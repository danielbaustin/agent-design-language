## Claim Posture

- Current: ADL has typed contracts, deterministic runtime surfaces, governed execution, evidence artifacts, and a repository-native C-SDLC.
- Current but bounded: multiple runtime generations and proof surfaces exist; they do not prove a complete autonomous society.
- Planned: a fully inhabited Cognitive Spacetime Manifold and the first true Gödel-agent birthday.
- Unknown: production adoption, external certification, and general performance superiority.

## Article Shape

1. The gap between a model response and an accountable action.

3. Why determinism surrounds rather than eliminates generative cognition.
4. What is implemented and what remains a roadmap.
5. Evidence-bound current limits and a bridge to the rest of the series.
