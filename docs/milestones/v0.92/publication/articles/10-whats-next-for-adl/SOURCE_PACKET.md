# Source Packet: What's Next for ADL

## Brief

- Audience: readers who followed the launch series and want a truthful roadmap.
- Promise: synthesize the implemented foundation, active v0.92 work, and longer-range questions.
- Angle: a roadmap is strongest when it distinguishes evidence, active work, proposals, and unknowns.
- Series role: close the arc without turning an active milestone into a release announcement.

## Claim Posture

- Current: ADL has accumulated typed language, runtime, governance, tool, security, social-cognition, and development-workflow foundations.
- Active: v0.92 is in development and its work packages remain evidence- and dependency-gated.
- Planned: first true Gödel-agent birthday, fuller inhabited runtime, Adaptive Learning DAG, CodeFriend alpha, and broader ecosystem work.
- Unknown: the final shape, timing, and real-world consequences of long-lived governed agents.

## Article Shape

1. What the series established.
2. The v0.92 identity and birthday challenge.
3. Learning, tools, security, social intelligence, and development workflow.
4. The evidence bar for stronger claims.
5. An invitation to inspect, test, and challenge the work.

## Guardrails

Do not claim v0.92 is released, the birthday has occurred, all work packages are complete, or future milestones are commitments with fixed dates.
