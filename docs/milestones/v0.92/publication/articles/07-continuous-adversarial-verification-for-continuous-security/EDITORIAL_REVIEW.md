# Editorial Review: Continuous Adversarial Verification

## Verdict

**Accept for series review.** The article explains continuous security as a governed evidence loop and avoids operational exploit detail or inflated assurance.

## Editorial Checks

- Lead: establishes why snapshot reviews cannot be the sole control for changing agent systems.
- Structure: accountable roles, loop, agent threat model, governance, memory, current boundary.
- Safety: the article discusses exploit attempts at an architectural level only.
- Series overlap: Freedom Gate and learning are referenced as dependencies, not repeated tutorials.

## Evidence And Claim Review

- Current claims are limited to architecture, bounded bridge surfaces, source packets, and reviewed evidence.
- No external certification or comprehensive production-defense claim appears.
- Failed exploit attempts are not treated as proof of absence.
- Broader adversarial execution is correctly described as later work.

## Privacy And Publication Review

No exploit payloads, live targets, credentials, customer findings, private paths, or sensitive traces appear. Nothing authorizes testing outside declared targets.

## Findings And Disposition

- Resolved: added explicit governance of defensive mutation as well as offensive testing.
- Resolved: strengthened the bounded-target and negative-result language.
- Residual: any future case study needs a separate redaction and target-authorization review.
