Smoke Sample Transcript

ChatGPT

Welcome to Agent Logic. Today we are meeting the AI coworkers: not as magic boxes, and not as replacements for people, but as strange new collaborators that can help us think, draft, question, and follow through.

Gemini

The practical difference is follow-through. A chatbot answers a prompt. An agent can keep track of a goal, use tools, check its work, and come back with evidence. That makes it useful, but it also means we need better habits for supervising it.

Claude

The human part matters most. A good AI coworker should make the room calmer and clearer. It should ask better questions, preserve context, and make the next action easier to choose.

ChatGPT

So the promise is not that AI makes judgment disappear. The promise is that routine coordination gets lighter, and people get more room for taste, intent, and responsibility.

Gemini

That is also why receipts matter. If an agent changes a file, buys compute, or publishes something, we need a plain record of what happened. Trust comes from visibility, not vibes.

Claude

Maybe the simplest test is this: after working with an AI system, do you feel more capable, or merely more impressed? The best tools leave people with more agency than they had before.
